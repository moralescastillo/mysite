I build and maintain the data layer that sits between your source systems and the people who need numbers: warehouses, pipelines, models and dashboards. I have been doing this since 2014, first in-house, now as a freelancer. My default is the smallest setup that does the job well.

### What I do

**Data warehouse setup and modelling.**
Design and implementation of a warehouse on Azure Synapse, BigQuery or Databricks, modelled with dbt. Includes naming conventions, testing, documentation and a deployment process your team can run without me.

**Pipelines and orchestration.**
Ingestion and transformation jobs with Airflow, Azure Data Factory or plain Python in containers. Monitoring and alerting so failures are seen before the Monday meeting.

**Dashboards and reporting.**
Power BI and Metabase dashboards built on a modelled layer, not on raw tables. Data marts, refresh automation, theming and the handover so analysts can extend them.

**Analysis.**
Descriptive, exploratory and predictive analysis in Python or R when a question needs an answer before it needs a dashboard.

**Audit and cleanup.**
A review of an existing stack: what breaks, what is slow, what nobody understands any more, and a prioritised plan to fix it.

### How an engagement works

Most projects start with a short discovery call, followed by a written scope with a fixed price or a day-rate estimate. For ongoing work I offer a monthly retainer with a set number of days.

Once we start, you get:

* a Calendly link to book calls at a time that suits you;
* a Kanban board (Trello or your own tool) where progress, blockers and deadlines are visible at all times;
* a shared folder (Google Drive or your own) for documentation and presentations;
* code in your repository, documented, from day one.

I keep calls on Tuesdays where possible so the rest of the week is uninterrupted work. I work remotely from Berlin and travel to clients in Germany when it helps.

### Tools I work with

dbt, Azure Synapse, Azure Data Factory, BigQuery, Google Cloud Composer, Airflow, Databricks, Docker, Python, R, SQL, Power BI, DAX, Metabase.

I am tool-agnostic. If your stack is different, the approach is the same.
